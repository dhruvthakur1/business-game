Design system v0.1

A financial terminal wrapped around a warm, hand-built world. The chrome stays cool ink so numbers read instantly; color lives in the buildings, tiers and leagues.

01 Numbers are the hero Every value in mono, right-aligned, with its unit and a trend arrow.
02 Always show the next rung Gap to the next rank, item or tier, with time-to-goal, on every screen.
03 Calm, not casino No fake urgency, no paid power. Alerts are rate-limited and opt-out.
