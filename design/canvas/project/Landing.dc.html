How it works

Every product is someone's input

Your bakery needs another player's flour. Their mill needs your wheat. Supply chains overlap, so trading is how everyone grows.

01 · Produce Set a job, come back later Buildings run 4-hour cycles. Better inputs make better outputs, so quality is earned through the chain, not bought.
02 · Trade Exchange, contracts and tenders Sell on an open order book or sign escrowed supply contracts. Deliver on time and your reliability score opens bigger deals.
03 · Grow Borrow, build, branch out The bank lends against your cash flow. Move up from food into energy, steel, electronics, cars and luxury goods with your own brand.
Compete

A league of 30 companies your size

Each week you're grouped with companies in your division. Top 7 move up, bottom 5 move down. Scores are normalized, so a small company having a great week can beat a big one.
